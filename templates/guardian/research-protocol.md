Research claim rules:

- Executing a protocol does not prove the hypothesis.
- Observed result must not be generalized beyond dataset/source constraints.
- Lack of contrary evidence is not confirmation.
- A literature summary is navigation/evidence context, not proof.
- If measurement method changes, prior permission and Plan validity become STALE.
- DEVIATED protocol results must state the deviation and lower claim ceiling.
